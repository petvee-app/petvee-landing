Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Tiếng Việt

PetVee ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you use the PetVee app.

1. Information we collect

When you use PetVee, we may collect:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you sign up.
  • Pet information: Name, breed, age, weight, sex, neutering status, and health data you enter through daily check-ins.
  • Photos: Images you upload to use the AI Disease Scanner feature.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the app, used to improve your experience.
  • Location (optional): When you use the clinic finder, with your permission.
  • Subscription information: The plan you purchased (Chăm Plan, Yêu Plan), its status and duration, and the transaction time. We do not receive or store your card number or payment details.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve PetVee services
  • Analyze pet health with AI (AI Vet and AI Disease Scanner)
  • Send vaccine reminders and health notifications
  • Show clinics near you
  • Verify and manage your subscription entitlement
  • Improve the user experience

3. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data when:

  • It is necessary to provide the service (for example, sending images to an AI API for analysis)
  • It is required by law enforcement or legal process
  • You choose to share it (for example, community posts)

4. Payments and subscriptions

When you purchase or restore a subscription, the transaction is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. PetVee does not receive or store your card or payment details.

To verify and manage your subscription entitlement, we share a minimal set of data with third-party payment and subscription management service providers: your PetVee account identifier, the purchased product ID, subscription status and expiry, and transaction time. These providers process the data only on our instructions and only to operate the subscription feature. We do not sell this data.

You can cancel auto-renewal at any time in your Google Play or Apple App Store subscription settings.

5. Data security

We apply reasonable security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (SSL/TLS) and database access controls.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Delete your account and all related data

7. Data storage

Your data is stored on secure servers through Supabase. We keep your data until you request deletion or delete your account.

8. Children

PetVee is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be communicated through the app or by email.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact: privacy@petvee.app
